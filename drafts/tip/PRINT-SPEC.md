# Officeworks Print & Copy counter, A6 tip sign

Approved by Daniele 26 Nov 2026.

File: `Tip Sign A6 - print ready.pdf` (exact A6, no bleed). It is on the USB drive root and in the emailed copy.

## Specs to give the counter

- Quantity: **1**, or **2** if you want a spare
- Size: **A6, 105 × 148 mm, portrait**
- Sides: **single sided**
- Colour: **full colour**
- Scale: **100%, actual size**. No fit to page, no shrink to fit
- Paper: **250 to 300 gsm matte or uncoated card**
- Finish: **no lamination**, no gloss. The A6 holder protects it and gloss would glare
- Bleed: **none needed**. The file is exactly A6 and the navy runs to the edge
- If they print on a larger sheet, ask them to **trim to 105 × 148 mm**
- Expected cost: a couple of dollars, not $23

## What to avoid

Do not use the **A6 Flyers** online product. Its minimum is a 50 pack at $23.00, which is waste for one sign.

The bleed file `Tip Sign A6 - officeworks bleed.pdf` is only for bulk flyer orders. You do not need it today.

## Check before you leave

- Deep brand navy right to the edge
- White "Enjoying the music?" and pale blue "Tap or scan to leave a tip"
- One large, sharp black QR on a white card with a pink outline
- White Daniele Buatti wordmark
- `danielebuatti.com/tip` along the bottom
