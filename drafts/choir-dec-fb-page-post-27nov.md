## Post

One song. Four parts. One Saturday morning.

Resonance Pop-Up Choir is back on Saturday 5 December, 10am to 1pm, at Armadale Baptist Church. This time we are singing Somewhere Only We Know by Keane, arranged for four-part harmony.

No choir experience and no music reading needed. I teach each part step by step at the piano, and we bring the harmonies together by the end of the morning. Printed sheet music, tea and water are all provided.

It is a song many of us already know, so we can spend less time learning the melody and more time discovering the harmonies, including the big build in the bridge.

Everyone is welcome. Resonance is an inclusive space for LGBTQIA+ people, allies and anyone who loves to sing. If cost is a barrier right now, please get in touch, I would love to have you there.

Tickets and details: https://events.humanitix.com/resonance-choir-dec
